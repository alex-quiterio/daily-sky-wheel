    Method

    Zodiac
    Tropical, geocentric. True node, mean Lilith.
    Planets
    Astronomy Engine, accurate to under 1′.
    Chiron & asteroids ≈
    Fixed orbital elements tuned to a reference chart (9 Nov 2026). Within a few arcminutes for 2024–2029, less accurate outside that.
    Houses
    Placidus falls back to Equal above 66° latitude.
    Chinese
    Lunar months and New Year follow China Standard Time. The month pillar turns at each solar term and the day pillar at local midnight, with no true-solar-time correction.
    Maya
    GMT correlation (584283): 21 Dec 2012 = 13.0.0.0.0 4 Ajaw 3 K'ank'in. The day turns at local midnight.
    Kabbalah
    Hebrew calendar by the fixed arithmetic rules; the day turns at local sunset. Candle lighting 18 minutes before sunset; Havdalah when the Sun is 8.5° below the horizon. Month correspondences after Sefer Yetzirah.
    Vedic
    Sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa, mean Rahu and Ketu, whole-sign houses from the Lagna, and amanta lunar months. The weekday (vara) turns at sunrise.
    Islamic
    Hijri months by the Umm al-Qura rule at Mecca; local moon sighting may differ by a day. The day turns at Maghrib. Prayer times by the chosen method, with an angle-based fallback at high latitudes.
    Times
    Shown in the selected place's local time.
